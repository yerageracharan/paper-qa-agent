# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation that is respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project. Be considerate of different experience levels and backgrounds. Critique ideas and implementation details, not people.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may remove comments or contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not include private research material, credentials, or unrelated personal information in a report.
